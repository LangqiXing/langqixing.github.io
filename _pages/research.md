    </h2>
    <p class="project__authors"><em><strong>L. Xing</strong>, S. Liu, Y. Ross</em></p>

    <ul class="project__bullets" lang="en">
      <li>Designed a conceptual process and techno-economic analysis to convert propane and bromine into 100 kta of propylene at Alkyl Products Limited Company</li>

    </ul>

      <li>使用 3 台 PFR、1 台电化学反应器与 6 座精馏塔</li>
      <li>测算 13 年运营后 NPV = 1800 万美元，NPV% = 0.69%，IRR = 12%</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="no-rule project__title">
      <span lang="en">Production of Benzene by Hydrodealkylation of Toluene</span>
      <span lang="zh">由甲苯加氢脱烷基生产苯</span>
    </h2>
    <p class="project__authors"><em><strong>L. Xing</strong>, S. Liu, Y. Ross</em></p>

    <ul class="project__bullets" lang="en">
      <li>Designed a conceptual process and techno-economic analysis to convert toluene and hydrogen into 100 kta of benzene at Alkyl Products Limited Company</li>
      <li>Used a PFR reactor and a separation system with 1 flash drum, 1 PSA, and 2 distillation columns</li>
      <li>Calculated project NPV = $108.6 MM, NPV% = 16, IRR = 33% after 13 years of operation</li>
    </ul>
    <ul class="project__bullets" lang="zh">
      <li>为 Alkyl Products Limited 公司设计了由甲苯与氢气生产 100 kta 苯的概念工艺及技术经济分析</li>
      <li>使用 PFR 反应器，分离系统包含 1 台闪蒸罐、1 台 PSA 和 2 座精馏塔</li>
      <li>测算 13 年运营后 NPV = 1.086 亿美元，NPV% = 16，IRR = 33%</li>
    </ul>

      <li>以水为溶剂，搭配多种表面活性剂、酸性组分、疏水涂层剂和泡沫稳定剂</li>
