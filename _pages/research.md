<h2>🤖 <em><span lang="en">Fabric Pneumatic Artificial Muscle with a Gripper</span><span lang="zh">织物气动人工肌肉与抓手</span></em></h2>
<p><em><strong>L. Xing</strong>, Y. Xi</em></p>

<ul lang="en">

</ul>

<h2>🧪 <em><span lang="en">Production of Propylene from Propane and Bromine</span><span lang="zh">由丙烷与溴生产丙烯</span></em></h2>
<p><em><strong>L. Xing</strong>, S. Liu, Y. Ross</em></p>

<ul lang="en">
  <li>Designed a conceptual process and techno-economic analysis for the conversion of propane and bromine to produce 100 kta of propylene at Alkyl Products Limited Company</li>
  <li>Used 3 PFRs, 1 electrochemical reactor, and 6 distillation columns</li>
  <li>Calculated project NPV = $18 MM, NPV% = 0.69%, IRR = 12% after 13 years of operation</li>
</ul>

<ul lang="zh">
  <li>为 Alkyl Products Limited 公司设计了由丙烷与溴生产 100 kta 丙烯的概念工艺及技术经济分析</li>
  <li>使用 3 台 PFR、1 台电化学反应器与 6 座精馏塔</li>
  <li>测算 13 年运营后 NPV = 1800 万美元，NPV% = 0.69%，IRR = 12%</li>
</ul>

<h2>🧪 <em><span lang="en">Production of Benzene by Hydrodealkylation of Toluene</span><span lang="zh">由甲苯加氢脱烷基生产苯</span></em></h2>
<p><em><strong>L. Xing</strong>, S. Liu, Y. Ross</em></p>

<ul lang="en">
  <li>Designed a conceptual process and techno-economic analysis for the conversion of toluene and hydrogen to produce 100 kta of benzene at Alkyl Products Limited Company</li>
  <li>Used a PFR reactor and a separation system consisting of 1 flash drum, 1 PSA, and 2 distillation columns</li>
  <li>Calculated project NPV = $108.6 MM, NPV% = 16, IRR = 33% after 13 years of operation</li>
</ul>

<ul lang="zh">
  <li>为 Alkyl Products Limited 公司设计了由甲苯与氢气生产 100 kta 苯的概念工艺及技术经济分析</li>
  <li>使用 PFR 反应器，分离系统包含 1 台闪蒸罐、1 台 PSA 和 2 座精馏塔</li>
  <li>测算 13 年运营后 NPV = 1.086 亿美元，NPV% = 16，IRR = 33%</li>
</ul>

<h2>🧹 <em><span lang="en">A Novel No-Scrub Window Cleaner for Skyscrapers</span><span lang="zh">面向摩天大楼的免擦拭洗窗剂</span></em></h2>
<p><em><strong>L. Xing</strong>, Y. Xi, G. Wang, D. Lai</em></p>

<ul lang="en">
  <li>Designed a novel non-scrub window cleaner for skyscrapers</li>
  <li>Used soft matter concepts to clean dust with diameters ranging from 1 to 100 µm</li>
  <li>Used water as a solvent, with various surfactants, acidic components, hydrophobic coating agents, and foam stabilizers</li>
  <li>Specified the ingredients, yield strength, and viscosity ranges in the cleaner</li>
</ul>

<ul lang="zh">
  <li>为摩天大楼设计了一款无需擦拭的洗窗剂</li>
  <li>借助软物质概念清除直径 1–100 µm 的灰尘</li>
  <li>以水为溶剂，搭配多种表面活性剂、酸性组分、疏水涂层剂和泡沫稳定剂</li>
  <li>明确了配方组分、屈服强度与粘度范围</li>
</ul>
